**First comment (maker):**
> Hey Product Hunt 👋
>
> I rebuilt oioi from the ground up. The old version was a native Swift app; this
> one is a clean Electron rewrite that's faster, prettier, and actually fun to use.
>
> What's new:
> • **Real liquid glass** — it captures the desktop behind the panel and blurs it,
>   so it's genuine macOS glass, not a flat overlay. Or pick a lightweight Soft
>   white panel.
> • **Instant** — ⌥V and it's already there.
> • **Text, images & files** — all captured, searchable, filterable by type/date.
> • **Tweakable** — blur, corner radius, transparency, your own shortcut.
> • **Free & open source**, lives in your menu bar, no account, no tracking.
>
> It's not paid-Apple-notarized (it's free!), so first launch needs a one-time

## Hacker News / Reddit (r/macapps)

**Title:** oioi – a free, open-source clipboard manager for macOS with real liquid-glass blur

**Body:** Rewrote my clipboard manager as an Electron app. The interesting bit: to
get real glass it screenshots the region behind the panel and blurs that in CSS
(macOS vibrancy can't be clipped to a custom radius, and `backdrop-filter` can't
reach the desktop). There's also a zero-permission "Soft" mode. Universal build,
no Xcode, MIT. Feedback welcome.

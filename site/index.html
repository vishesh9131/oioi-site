01 — GLASS

Real liquid glass

oioi screenshots the desktop behind the panel and blurs that — genuine glass, not a flat gray overlay faking it. Or pick a clean, zero-permission Soft panel.

02 — SPEED

Opens before you blink

The panel is pre-warmed off-screen and painted low-res first. Press ⌥V and your whole history is already floating in front of you.

03 — EVERYTHING

Text, images & files

Everything you copy is captured, thumbnailed and instantly searchable. Filter to exactly the type you need — today or all-time — and one click pastes it back.

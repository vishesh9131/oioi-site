Features

Small app. Big memory.

oioi keeps everything you copy and gives it back the instant you ask — without ever getting in your way.

Text, images & files

Everything you copy is captured, thumbnailed and searchable. Filter by type — today or all-time.

Instant by design

Pre-warmed and rendered low-res first. Hit ⌥V and your history is already in front of you.

Private by default

History lives only on your machine. No cloud, no account, no telemetry — nothing leaves your device.

Glass or Soft

Choose a frosted liquid-glass panel or a clean flat one. Tune the look and set your own shortcut.

Every desktop

Native builds for macOS — Apple Silicon and Intel — plus Windows and Linux. One app, everywhere.

Free & open source

No subscription, no upsell, no tracking. MIT-licensed — every line is on GitHub to read and fork.

How it works

Three keys, then it's gone.

No windows to manage. Summon it, take what you need, and it disappears.

01

⌥V  Summon

Hit the shortcut from any app. The panel floats up over whatever you're doing.

02

Search & filter

Type to find anything you've copied, or filter by text, images or files.

03

↵  Paste

Enter drops it back on your clipboard, ready to paste. The panel vanishes.
